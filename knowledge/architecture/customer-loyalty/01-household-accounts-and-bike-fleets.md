# Household Accounts and Bike Fleets

## Household Account Hierarchy
The POS Customer Profiles module supports a household-level grouping of customer records. A Household Account consists of a **Primary Account Holder** and linked **Household Dependent/Family Profiles** (e.g., Spouse, Children, Partners). This hierarchical model ensures that related individuals are organized under a single household umbrella while maintaining individual profiles for personalized tracking and communication.

## Unified Bike Fleet View
The point-of-sale register includes a unified lookup component designed to provide a comprehensive view of all `bike_asset` records owned by members of the household on a single screen. This allows staff to quickly identify and select the correct bicycle during service intake or trade-in evaluations (e.g., *"Which bike are we servicing today? Dad's Diverge or Maya's Riprock?"*). This feature streamlines the customer experience and ensures accurate tracking of service histories per asset.

## Shared Family Assets & Permissions
Household accounts are empowered with shared resources and permissions:
- **Shared Store Credit Balances:** Family members within the same household can pool and share store credit balances.
- **Redeem Household Loyalty Rewards:** Accumulated loyalty rewards can be redeemed by any authorized family member.
- **Service Authorizations:** Family members have the authority to approve repair work orders and estimates on any bike belonging to the household fleet.

This collaborative approach to account management mirrors real-world family dynamics and enhances customer convenience across all touchpoints.
