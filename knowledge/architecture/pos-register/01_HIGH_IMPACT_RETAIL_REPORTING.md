# High-Impact Retail & Service Reporting Specifications

- **Vendor Reliability Index**: Real-time metrics on vendor lead times, backorder rates, and shipping accuracy.
- **Mechanic Labor Efficiency & Margin**: Tracking billable labor hours vs. clock time, average turn time, and revenue per mechanic.
- **True GMROI & Velocity Reordering**: Reorder alerts based on Gross Margin Return on Investment and vendor lead times.
