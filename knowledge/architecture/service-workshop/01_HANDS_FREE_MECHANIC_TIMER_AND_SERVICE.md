# Hands-Free Mechanic Service Engine & Online Booking

- **Hands-Free Mechanic Labor Timer**: Mobile/tablet companion app paired to mechanics' phones with quick buttons and voice-command support ("Start timer Workorder #1042", "Add note: chain worn 0.75%").
- **Workorder Aging & Service Tracking**: Real-time dashboard tracking "Days in Service" with alert badges for stale repairs.
- **Online Workorder Reservations**: Customer-facing booking portal for scheduling service drop-off windows and pre-allocating shop labor capacity.
